In the Beginning Was the Word

Before anything was made, the Word already was — with God, and was God. Everything that exists came through Him, and no darkness that has ever risen has been able to put His light out.

Word and Faith Network · John 1:1–3, 5
"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. The same was in the beginning with God. All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made... And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not."
John 1:1-3, 5, KJV

Every story has a starting point, and John's Gospel reaches further back than any of them to find His. Genesis opens with "In the beginning God created." John opens with "In the beginning was the Word" — and then quietly tells you the Word and God were never two separate things to begin with. Before the first morning, before the first light, before anything had a name, the Word already was. Not becoming. Not beginning. Was. John is not warming up to a story about Jesus. He is telling you who was already there before the story needed a first page.

The Word Was Already There

"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God." Read it slowly and you'll notice John refuses to let you pick a side. The Word was with God — distinct enough to be in relationship. The Word was God — one enough in nature that no gap exists between Them. John isn't being careless with his grammar. He's protecting a truth the rest of the chapter is going to build on: Jesus Christ did not arrive in Bethlehem as a new idea God suddenly had. He was there before "there" existed.

This matters for you today because it means nothing you're facing caught Him off guard. He was not created to respond to your crisis. He was already present when the very fabric your crisis is happening inside of was spoken into being. Whatever is in front of you right now, it is not older than Him. It is not wiser than Him. It did not exist one second before He did.

Everything That Exists Came Through Him

"All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made." John doesn't hedge this. Not most things. Not the visible things. All things — and then he says it again from the other direction just to close every loophole: without Him, nothing that has ever been made, was made. Every mountain, every cell, every law that holds an atom together, every person who has ever drawn breath — all of it passed through His hands on its way into existence.

"For by him were all things created, that are in heaven, and that are in earth, visible and invisible... and he is before all things, and by him all things consist."Colossians 1:16-17, KJV

Paul says it from a different angle but lands in the same place: Christ isn't just the one who made everything — He's the one holding everything together right now, including whatever in your life currently feels like it's coming apart. The hands that formed the world have not grown tired, and they have not let go.

The Light the Darkness Could Never Catch

"And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not." That last word carries two meanings at once, and both are true. The darkness did not understand the Light — it couldn't make sense of something that operates by a completely different nature than itself. And the darkness did not overcome the Light either — every attempt to extinguish it has failed, all the way from a Roman tomb sealed with a stone to every private battle you have ever fought in secret. Darkness is loud. Darkness is relentless. But darkness has never once, in the history of this verse being true, managed to put this Light out.

"In him was life; and the life was the light of men."John 1:4, KJV

If you feel surrounded right now — pressed in by circumstances, by a diagnosis, by a delay, by something you can't fix on your own — this is the verse to stand on. The Light already shines in darkness. Not after the darkness clears. In it. Right now, exactly where you are.

The Word for Today

Jesus was not an afterthought, and He is not improvising with your situation. He was there in the beginning, with God and as God. Everything that has ever existed, including whatever you're holding onto right now, was made by Him and is still held together by Him. And the darkness trying to close in on you — whatever shape it's taking today — has never once succeeded in putting His light out, and it will not start with you. Let the Word that made everything be the Word you build today on.
