Potential in the Holy Ghost

You are not waiting on power. Power moved in the day you got saved and has been waiting on you to act like it.

Word and Faith Network · Acts 1:8
"But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judaea, and in Samaria, and unto the uttermost part of the earth."
Acts 1:8, KJV

I need somebody to sit up and hear this, because it is not a suggestion — it is the truth about what moved into you the day you got saved. You are not a believer with potential. You are a believer carrying it. The disciples in Acts 1 were not told to go find power somewhere out there. They were told power was coming upon them — and once it landed, it stayed. That same power did not visit the early church and leave. It relocated. It took up residence. And if the Holy Ghost lives in you, then everything He is capable of is no longer a question of availability. It is only a question of activation.

Say that back to yourself: not availability — activation. God is not holding anything back from you. You are holding something back from the world.

You Are Not Waiting For Power. You're Carrying It.

Most of us pray like Pentecost hasn't happened yet. We beg God for strength we were already given. We ask for boldness that is already sitting in us, unopened, like a gift nobody bothered to unwrap. The disciples waited in that upper room for ten days — not because power was slow, but because they needed to be in one accord before they could carry what was coming without it tearing them apart. God is not slow getting power to you. You are sometimes slow getting positioned to receive what He already sent.

So stop praying "God, give me power" when the Word already told you what you have. Start praying "God, show me where to spend it." That is a completely different prayer, and it will take you somewhere completely different.

The Holy Ghost Is Not a Feeling — He's a Force

The word "power" in Acts 1:8 is the Greek word dunamis — the same root that gives us the word dynamite. Not a warm sensation in a worship service. Not goosebumps when the music swells. Explosive, inherent, world-moving ability. That is what came upon the church. And it did not come upon them to make them feel something — it came upon them to make them do something: be witnesses, in Jerusalem, in Judea, in Samaria, and to the uttermost part of the earth. The feeling was never the destination. The feeling was the ignition.

"Now unto him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that worketh in us,"Ephesians 3:20, KJV

Read that verse slowly. Not according to the power that exists somewhere out there. According to the power that worketh in us. Present tense. Still working. Still available. Still yours. You keep asking God for a bigger miracle when He already deposited more than your biggest ask inside you the moment the Holy Ghost moved in.

Potential Untapped Is Still Potential Wasted

Here is the hard part, and I will not soften it: a seed that is never planted does not get credit for what it could have grown into. It just rots with its potential still folded up inside it. The Holy Ghost in you is the same way. He does not do anything in you that you refuse to let Him do through you. Unused power is not humble. It is wasted. You were not filled so you could sit quietly and feel spiritual. You were filled so that when fear shows up, you move anyway; when the door looks closed, you pray anyway; when everybody else shrinks, you stand anyway — because something bigger than your nerves is living in your chest.

Stop measuring your life by your natural capacity. Your natural capacity was never the whole story once the Holy Ghost came upon you. Measure it by what He is able to do — and then act like you believe it.

He Didn't Fill You to Watch You Stay the Same

Notice the order in that verse: power first, witnesses second. God does not send you into anything you don't already have the equipment for. If He has called you to speak, He has already given you the boldness. If He has called you to lead, He has already given you the strength. If He has called you to forgive, to rebuild, to start over, to stand in a room nobody thinks you belong in — the power came before the assignment, not after. You are not waiting to be ready. You were made ready the day the Holy Ghost moved in.

"For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind."2 Timothy 1:7, KJV

So whatever has been making you shrink — that spirit did not come from Him. He gave you power. He gave you love. He gave you a sound mind. Fear was never on that list, and it has no legal right to the house you're living in now.

The Word for Today

Somebody needed to hear this today: you are not under-resourced. You are under-deployed. The same power that split a sea, raised a dead man, and shook a prison off its foundations is not a story in your Bible — it is a resident in your spirit. Stop asking God for what He already gave you and start walking like you have it, because you do. The potential was never the problem. Your permission to use it was. So give yourself permission. The Holy Ghost already gave you the power. Today, go be the witness.
