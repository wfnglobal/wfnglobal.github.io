The Word, Faith, and Prayer

Our foundation — and why Word and Faith Network exists.

Word and Faith Network · Foundational Teaching
"So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God."
Romans 10:17 — KJV

Every ministry stands on something. Ours stands on three things that are really one thing: the Word of God, received by faith, activated through prayer. This is not a slogan — it is how the Christian life actually works, and it is what Word and Faith Network exists to teach, model, and practice daily.

The Word: Our Foundation

We believe the Bible is the inspired, inerrant, and final word of God to mankind. It is not a collection of suggestions or ancient opinions — it is alive and active in every generation, including this one.

"All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness."2 Timothy 3:16, KJV

The Word is "quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword" (Hebrews 4:12). This means something practical: when the Word speaks, the conversation is over. Whatever the circumstance says, whatever the diagnosis says, whatever the bank account says — the Word of God has the final say. We do not build our confession on our feelings or our circumstances. We build it on what is written.

Faith: Our Response

Faith is not a feeling we work up. It is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen (Hebrews 11:1) — and it has a specific source.

"So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God."Romans 10:17, KJV

You cannot manufacture faith by trying harder. You grow faith by filling yourself with the Word until what God says becomes louder in you than what you see. This is why WFN will never separate "faith content" from the Word. Every declaration we publish, every prayer we pray, is rooted in a specific scripture — because faith without the Word behind it is just positive thinking, and we are not in the business of positive thinking. We are in the business of believing God.

And faith is not passive. "Faith without works is dead" (James 2:17). Real faith acts on what it believes — it prays, it obeys, it moves.

Prayer: Our Access

If the Word is the foundation and faith is the response, prayer is where it all becomes real. Prayer is not primarily about asking God for things — it is covenant communication with the God who has already made promises, and who invites us to lay hold of them.

"Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you."Matthew 7:7, KJV

We believe in bold, specific, scripture-based prayer — not vague wishing, but declaration rooted in what God has already said. "The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much" (James 5:16). This is why our prayers are never generic. They name the battle, they stand on the promise, and they declare the outcome before it is seen — because that is how faith prays.

How It Works Together

The Word produces faith. Faith empowers prayer. Prayer activates the promises of the Word in our lives, our families, and our nations. This is the cycle Word and Faith Network was built to serve — not a formula to manipulate God, but a pattern scripture itself teaches, lived out daily, together, by believers across the world who may never meet in person but who stand on the same Word, pray the same kind of prayers, and carry the same faith.

This is what we believe. This is why we exist. And this is the Word we bring to you — every single day.
