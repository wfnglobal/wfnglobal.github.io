The Word

Everything starts here. Word and Faith Network was not built around personality or performance — it was built around one verse: “So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.” Scripture isn't background noise to what we do. It is the entire ministry.

Every sermon we preach, every teaching we publish, every line of every prayer traces back to something God has already said. We open the Word first, and let it set the agenda.

That's why the app carries a complete, offline King James Bible, and why nothing we publish is ever taken down. Read today's Word, then go back as far as you want — it will still be here.

Prayer

Prayer, here, is not a ritual — it's a weapon and a relationship. Every morning we post a prophetic declaration: short, punchy, scripture-rooted lines meant to be spoken out loud, not just read silently and scrolled past.

We don't pray generic prayers and hope something lands. We pray targeted, faith-filled intercession — because we believe God still answers specific prayers for specific people in specific seasons.

Join the weekly Prayer Circle, live on X Spaces, or the daily WhatsApp community carrying intercession and Bible study between sessions. You were never meant to carry your prayer list alone.

Faith

Faith is not wishful thinking. It's not crossing your fingers and hoping for the best. Scripture calls it “the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen” — a lifestyle rooted in what God has said, not in what circumstances suggest.

Our teaching exists to move a reader from simply knowing about God to actually walking in His promises. That's the whole purpose of the Library — full books, published free, every two weeks, studied slowly, the way the Bereans studied: searching the Scriptures daily to see if what they'd heard was so.

Faith cometh by hearing. Hearing produces believing. Believing produces living differently — one Word, one prayer, one teaching at a time.

No Building, No Address

One Word, reaching every nation at once.

WFN has no sanctuary and no congregation in one place — just believers everywhere, gathering daily around the same Word, the same prayer, the same declaration. Every color on that circle is a nation, a tongue, a timezone. The network is the church.

Like Joseph's coat, it was never meant to be one color. It was meant to be worn by someone who stood out — favored, sent, and faithful wherever he landed.

Today's Declaration

Father, before I ask You for one more thing this morning, I invite You in — search me, and know my heart! Try me, and know my thoughts! Nothing hidden, nothing held back!

I will not perform a prayer today. I will not dress up my words and call it faith. I come as I am, and I let You look as deep as You want to look!

So I command every wicked way in me to come to the surface now — in Jesus' name — where grace can reach it and mercy can deal with it!

I declare: I am not afraid of what Your light exposes, because Your light is not here to condemn me — it is here to lead me! Lead me, O God, in the way everlasting!

Every motive I haven't questioned, every habit I've excused, every attitude I've defended — I command it into the open right now, in Jesus' name!

I will not carry hidden things into a new day and call it victory. What I hide, I cannot heal. What I surrender, God can transform!

So this morning I command my heart to be honest, my spirit to be teachable, and my day to be built on truth — not image, not appearance, truth!

If something in you has been avoiding this kind of prayer — hear this: God already sees it. The only thing hiding does is keep you from the help!

Say it before the day gives you a reason to perform instead of surrender. Search me, O God. Know my heart. Try me, and know my thoughts. Lead me in the way everlasting. In Jesus' name, amen!

Psalm 139:23-24
