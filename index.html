Who We Are

Built on the Word. Fueled by Prayer. Standing in Faith.

Word and Faith Network is a gospel ministry dedicated to one thing: giving believers around the world a daily encounter with the Word of God.

We believe faith is not wishful thinking — it is a lifestyle rooted in scripture, sustained by prayer, and proven by God's track record. That is why everything we publish is anchored in the Bible, written for people who are serious about their walk with Christ.

We have no building and no address — and we don't need one. Wherever two or three gather in His name, in person or across a screen, that is enough. WFN exists to make that gathering possible, every day, for anyone, anywhere.

Our core belief is simple: the Word produces faith, and faith prays. Scripture is not background noise to our ministry — it is the entire ministry. Every prayer we write and every teaching we share traces back to something God has already said. Read our full statement of belief on the Word, Faith, and Prayer →

Three Pillars

Every Day. Three Pillars. One Mission.

Everything WFN publishes belongs to one of these three streams — so you always know what you're receiving, and why.

The Word

Scripture delivered daily — not just to be read, but declared. We carry God's promises from the page into your mouth, your home, your situation.

Prayer

Targeted, faith-filled prayers written from the Word of God — specific declarations over your life, your family, your health, your nation.

Faith

Bible studies and teachings designed to move you from knowing about God to knowing God — and walking in everything He has promised.
