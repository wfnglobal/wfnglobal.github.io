A Word for the Season

Welcome to the family — here's what's happening at Word & Faith Network.

From Oladapo

If you're reading this, you just joined a family of believers who refuse to go a day without the Word. We're glad you're here. Word and Faith Network exists for one reason: to win souls and to help them with their burdens — through prayer, Bible verses, sermons, and books.

Every single day, we publish a fresh Verse of the Day, a full sermon, and a Declaration you can speak over your life out loud. No subscription, no paywall, no fine print. Just the Word, delivered daily, for as long as you need it.

This Week at WFN

Today's sermon, "In the Beginning Was the Word," walks through John 1:1–3, 5 — the truth that before anything was made, the Word already was, with God and as God, and that no darkness that has ever risen has been able to put His light out.

"For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart."Hebrews 4:12, KJV — Today's Verse of the Day

And today's Declaration is built on exactly that: that the Word of God is alive and working in you right now, piercing through every lie, discerning the thoughts and intents of your heart, and holding your life together the same way it holds the universe together.

What's Growing

The WFN app keeps getting better — a cleaner Home screen, a Bible reader with tap-to-define words and translation into Yorùbá, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin and more, and "The Word Climb," a five-level Bible quiz game you can play and share. On Instagram and X, we're posting daily prayers and sermons — real, full-length prayers worth saving and sharing with someone who needs them.
