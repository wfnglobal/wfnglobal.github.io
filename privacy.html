Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Word and Faith Network ("WFN," "we," "us") publishes wfnglobal.org and the WFN app. This page explains what information we collect from visitors and how it's used. We keep this simple on purpose — we are a ministry, not a data company.

What We Collect

  • Information you give us directly: your name and email address if you join our WhatsApp prayer group, sign up for updates, or email us.
  • Information stored on your own device: the app saves your notes, highlighted verses, saved prayers, and reading activity directly in your browser's local storage, on your own device. We do not receive or store this on a server — it stays with you, and clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Basic site analytics: if enabled, we may use a standard analytics tool (such as Google Analytics) to understand which pages are visited and roughly how many people visit, so we know what's helping people and what isn't. This may use cookies.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.
  • We don't use the content of your personal notes or prayers for anything — they live on your device, not ours.

Third-Party Services

We use a small number of outside services to run this site and app: GitHub Pages (hosting), Mailchimp (email list management, if you sign up), and an analytics provider (to understand site traffic). Each of these has its own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review if you'd like more detail.

Children

WFN's content is suitable for a general audience, but the site and app are not directed at or intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can clear any data the app has saved on your device at any time through your browser's settings. You can unsubscribe from any email list at any time using the link in that email, or by contacting us directly.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Continuing to use the site or app after a change means you accept the update.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email us at wordandfaithnetwork@gmail.com.
