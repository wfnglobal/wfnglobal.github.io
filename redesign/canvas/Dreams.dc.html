Dreams & Discernment

A strange dream, a nightmare, a recurring one you can’t shake. You don’t have to work it out alone, and you don’t have to be afraid of it.

What Scripture says

God has spoken through dreams, to Joseph, to Daniel, and in the outpouring of the Spirit. Scripture is just as clear that not every dream is a message. Discernment, not panic.
