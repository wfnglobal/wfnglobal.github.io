The Lawful Captive Shall Go Free

Some captivity looks legal. You signed for it, you walked into it, the enemy has the paperwork. Heaven’s answer is still the same.

9 min read · Listen 11 min

“Shall the prey be taken from the mighty, or the lawful captive delivered? But thus saith the LORD, Even the captives of the mighty shall be taken away, and the prey of the terrible shall be delivered: for I will contend with him that contendeth with thee, and I will save thy children.”

Isaiah 49:24–25 · KJV

Before God ever answers the question in verse 24, Zion asks a sadder one in verse 14: “The LORD hath forsaken me, and my Lord hath forgotten me.” That is what exile sounds like from the inside. Not a theology debate. A tired sigh. And God’s reply is one of the tenderest lines in the whole Bible: “Behold, I have graven thee upon the palms of my hands.” Your name is not on a list somewhere in heaven. It is carved into His hands.

The Question Behind Your Silence

“Shall the prey be taken from the mighty?” That is not unbelief. That is honesty. Israel was looking at Babylon, the superpower of the age, the empire that didn’t give anything back. Can this addiction really let go of me? Can this debt really be broken? Can my son really come back from where he has gone?

You are allowed to bring Him the thing that looks impossible. What you are not allowed to do is let the size of the captor become the size of your God.
