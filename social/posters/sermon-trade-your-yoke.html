Matthew 11:28-30 · Rest for the weary

Lay it down.Take His yoke.

Jesus never told the weary to carry nothing — He offered a trade: your crushing yoke for His easy one, and rest unto your souls.

“Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”Matthew 11:28
