A prayer for the promise you keep visiting instead of living in. 🔑

You can quote it. You can recite the verse, screenshot the prophecy, remind yourself on the hard days that God already said it's yours. But quoting a promise and living inside it are two different addresses — and you've been commuting between them for a while now.

Tonight's prayer is for the tourist-with-a-brochure season: standing at the edge of your own inheritance, admiring it, leaving before dark. God didn't sign the deed so you'd keep it framed. ⬆️ Pray the full prayer in the image — and let something in you finally move in.

"Thou shalt possess them; and the house of Jacob shall possess their possessions." Obadiah 1:17
