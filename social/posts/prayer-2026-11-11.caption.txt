A prayer for when the people who should defend you start blaming you instead 🛡️🙏

Losing something is hard enough. Losing it and then watching the people closest to you go looking for someone to blame — and landing on you — is a different kind of hard.

David knew that exact moment. Ziklag was ashes, his family was gone, and the very men who'd fought beside him started talking about stoning him instead of standing with him. He didn't wait for them to come around. He went to God first.

Tonight's prayer is for the camp that turned on you before the dust even settled. Pray it as your own. ⬆️ Full prayer in the image.

"And David was greatly distressed; for the people spake of stoning him, because the souls of all the people were grieved, every man for his sons and for his daughters: but David encouraged himself in the LORD his God." 1 Samuel 30:6
