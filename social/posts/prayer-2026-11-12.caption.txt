A prayer for the one who wants to stop asking 🙏

You've asked so many times you're starting to feel foolish asking again. Same request, different month. Same door, still closed. You're tempted to stop walking back to the shore before the sixth no quietly becomes the reason you give up on the seventh.

God never once told the servant on Carmel he was asking too much. He just said go again — as if six nothings were simply the cost of getting to the cloud. Whatever you've been watching for without seeing it move, tonight isn't the night to stop looking.

"For the vision is yet for an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry." Habakkuk 2:3
