A prayer for the one everyone calls strong. 💪🕊️

You're the one everyone calls when it's falling apart — and somewhere in all that calling, nobody thought to ask who's holding you up.

Jesus never told the weary to perform strength until they collapsed. He said come, heavy laden, exactly as you are — and trade the yoke you built for yourself for the one that was actually made to fit.

"Cast thy burden upon the LORD, and he shall sustain thee: he shall never suffer the righteous to be moved." Psalm 55:22
