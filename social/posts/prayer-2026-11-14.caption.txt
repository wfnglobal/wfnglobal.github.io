A prayer for the gift you keep calling too small to matter ✋🔥

You've got the idea with no funding, the gift nobody's clapped for yet, the one talent you've been quietly burying under "not now" — and you've decided it's too small to bring to God until it looks more impressive.

Moses thought the same thing about the stick in his hand. God didn't ask him to go find something bigger first. He just asked him to look down at what he already had.
