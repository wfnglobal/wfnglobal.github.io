Lord, I keep describing the bones instead of speaking to them 🦴🔥

You know every bone in your valley by name — the marriage that's gone quiet, the dream you buried under a hundred disappointments, the body that doesn't answer the way it used to. Somewhere along the way you stopped praying over it and started narrating it instead, like a tour guide pointing at ruins, as if describing the damage carefully was the same thing as believing God could still do something with it.

Today's prayer is for the difference between grieving what's dry and giving up on it — for doing what Ezekiel did: standing in the middle of what looks finished and speaking to it anyway, before you see anything move.

"Thus saith the Lord GOD unto these bones; Behold, I will cause breath to enter into you, and ye shall live." Ezekiel 37:5
