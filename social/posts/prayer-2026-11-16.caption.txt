When you've prayed for it to go and it's still here 🕯️🙏

Maybe it's the diagnosis that didn't change after you believed it would. The marriage that's still hard after you forgave for the hundredth time. The habit, the grief, the ache in your body that answers to nothing you've tried. You've asked, and asked again, and it's still here — and somewhere in there you started wondering if that means God stopped listening.

Paul asked three times too. Heaven didn't take the thorn away. It gave him something better than removal — enough grace to carry it, and a strength that only shows up where your own runs out. The thorn staying doesn't mean you were skipped. It means there's room here for a power that isn't yours. In Jesus' name, amen.

"My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness." — 2 Corinthians 12:9

🙏 Pray: Lord, let Your grace be enough for me today, right where I am, with the thing that hasn't moved. In Jesus' name, amen.

Save this for the prayer you've prayed more than three times.
