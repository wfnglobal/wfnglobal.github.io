When you can't explain why this happened — yet 🕯️🙏

Maybe it's the job that fell apart, the friend who turned on you, the season that's dragged on so long you've stopped asking why. You didn't do anything to deserve it, and nobody's handed you an explanation. You've just been left holding something that still doesn't make sense.

Joseph carried that same silence for thirteen years — sold by his own brothers, forgotten in a cell, with no idea any of it was leading anywhere good. He didn't get the reason while he was living it. He only saw it looking back, standing in a place he never could have reached the easy way. Whatever you're carrying that still doesn't add up, it isn't wasted — it's being turned, right now, toward something you haven't seen yet. In Jesus' name, amen.

"Ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good." — Genesis 50:20

🙏 Pray: Lord, help me trust what You're doing before I can see it. In Jesus' name, amen.
