When you worked all night and still have nothing to show for it 🎣🌅

Peter was a professional. He knew that lake better than anyone standing on the shore, and he'd still fished all night and caught nothing. Then a carpenter who'd never thrown a net in His life told him to try again — in broad daylight, in front of the same crowd that had just watched him fail.

Lord, I've done everything I know how to do and I'm still coming up empty — the applications, the attempts, the nights I stayed up trying one more angle on a problem that won't move. I'm not asking You to explain why the first ten tries didn't work. I'm asking for the grace to try the eleventh one, because You're the One telling me to.

Help me hear Your voice over my own exhaustion, over the part of me that already knows how this usually goes. Let me say what Peter said instead of what I feel: nevertheless, at Thy word, I will let down the net. Whatever You're asking me to do again today that already failed once, give me the strength to do it anyway. In Jesus' name, amen.

"Nevertheless at thy word I will let down the net." — Luke 5:5

🙏 Pray: Lord, when You tell me to try again, let me obey before I understand why. In Jesus' name, amen.
