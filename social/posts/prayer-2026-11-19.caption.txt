When you're the reason you fell, and that's almost harder to carry than the fall itself 💔

Micah doesn't pretend it was all someone else's doing. He says it plainly — I will bear the indignation of the Lord, because I have sinned against him. No excuses, no blaming the situation. But in the very next breath he says something the enemy wasn't ready to hear: when I fall, I shall arise.

Lord, I know some of what knocked me down, I walked into myself — the choice I made twice knowing better, the door I kept opening after You'd already closed it. I'm not asking You to pretend otherwise. I'm asking for what Micah asked for: the strength to get back up anyway, because staying down doesn't undo what I did, it just adds shame on top of it.

So today I'm done explaining my fall to anyone who's enjoying the view. When I fall, I shall arise. When I sit in darkness, You will be the light — not my own effort, not my own excuses, just You, showing up for someone who fell and decided to stand up anyway. In Jesus' name, amen.

"When I fall, I shall arise; when I sit in darkness, the LORD shall be a light unto me." — Micah 7:8

🙏 Pray: Lord, when I fall, let me rise — and let You be the light I stand up into. In Jesus' name, amen.
