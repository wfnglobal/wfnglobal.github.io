That friendly voice that keeps asking you to "just stop by for a minute" — and somehow it's never actually a minute 📞😅

Nehemiah was building a wall with half his city still exposed, and four separate times a man named Sanballat sent him the exact same invitation dressed up as reasonableness: come down, let's talk, let's meet in the plain of Ono. It never once looked like an attack. It looked like a coffee chat. It looked like "you can always finish tomorrow."

Lord, I know that voice. It doesn't sound dangerous, it sounds friendly — a phone call, a scroll, a "can you just..." that always seems to land the moment I finally sit down to do what You called me to do. Today I'm asking for what Nehemiah had: the clarity to see the invitation for what it is, and the courage to give the same answer every single time it comes back dressed differently. I am doing a great work, so that I cannot come down. Guard my hands, guard my hours, and don't let me trade the wall for a conversation that was never really about peace. In Jesus' name, amen.

"I am doing a great work, so that I cannot come down." — Nehemiah 6:3

🙏 Pray: Lord, when the invitation to come down comes again today, give me the same answer Nehemiah gave — and the peace to mean it. In Jesus' name, amen.
