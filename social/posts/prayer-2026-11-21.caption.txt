When bad news lines up outside your door before you've even had coffee 😮‍💨🔥

The servant woke up early, stepped outside, and saw an entire army surrounding the city. He ran back in shaking, sure it was over. He wasn't wrong about what he saw — he was just the only one in that house who hadn't seen the rest of it yet.

Lord, some mornings everything shows up against me at the same time — the bill, the test result, the silence from someone I needed to hear from, all surrounding me before I've even had coffee. I don't want to keep describing the army. I want what the servant got: eyes open to what's already fighting for me. Elisha wasn't surrounded by less danger than his servant saw — he was surrounded by more help than his servant could see yet. Open my eyes the same way, Lord, and let me walk into today steady, not shaking, because what's around me is bigger than what's against me. In Jesus' name, amen.

"Fear not: for they that be with us are more than they that be with them." — 2 Kings 6:16

🙏 Pray: Lord, before I count what's against me again, open my eyes to what's already surrounding me for my good. In Jesus' name, amen.
