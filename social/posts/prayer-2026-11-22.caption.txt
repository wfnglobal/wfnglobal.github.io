You've been sowing in secret and wondering if God even noticed. 🌾

Lord, some of what I'm carrying right now isn't failure — it's seed. I said yes when it cost me something I couldn't really spare. I gave when my own hands were empty. I forgave someone who never even asked me to. And it's been quiet ever since, long enough that I've started to wonder if any of it actually mattered to You.

But Your word says the man who went out weeping, carrying seed he could have eaten for supper, came home singing with his arms full of sheaves. So I bring You my tears over what I've planted — don't let them be wasted, let them water it instead. Let what I buried in obedience keep growing where I can't see it yet, until the day I'm holding the harvest. In Jesus' name, amen.

"They that sow in tears shall reap in joy." — Psalm 126:5
