Ever lie awake wondering if God even notices the storm you're in? 🌊😔

The disciples weren't wrong that there was a real storm — the boat was taking on water and nobody was pretending otherwise. What stung more than the waves was that Jesus seemed to be sleeping through it. That's usually when the question comes out sideways: does He even care?

Lord, some nights the wind doesn't stop and I lie here running through everything that could go wrong. I've wondered more than once if You've noticed, or if You're just letting me sink. But You were never asleep to my need — You were waiting for the moment that actually mattered. So I'm asking You now: speak over my mind the same three words You spoke over that sea. Peace, be still. Let the panic go quiet because You said so, not because the circumstances changed. In Jesus' name, amen.

"Peace, be still." — Mark 4:39

🙏 Pray: Lord, speak peace, be still over my mind the way You spoke it over the sea — let the panic go quiet because You said so.
