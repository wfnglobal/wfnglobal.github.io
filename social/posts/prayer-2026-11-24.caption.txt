Ever waited so long for something to change that you quietly stopped expecting it to? 😔🕯️

There was a man by the pool at Bethesda who'd been sick for thirty-eight years. Long enough that he'd stopped racing for the water when it stirred — somebody always got there first. Long enough that when Jesus asked him a strange question — wilt thou be made whole? — he didn't even answer yes. He just explained why he couldn't.

Lord, I've been by this same pool so long that waiting has started to feel like who I am. I've gotten good at explaining why nothing has changed instead of believing it still can. Ask me again if I want to be whole, and when I say yes, don't just comfort me — tell me to rise. Give me the strength to pick up what's kept me down and walk. In Jesus' name, amen.

"Rise, take up thy bed, and walk." — John 5:8

🙏 Pray: Lord, ask me again if I want to be whole, and give me strength to rise when You do.

Save this for the wait that's gone on long enough to feel normal.
