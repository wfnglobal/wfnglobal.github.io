Ever gotten the answer you prayed for and just... kept walking? 🙏😳

Ten people with the same disease cried out for mercy on the same road, and all ten got healed on the spot. Only one of them turned around to say thank you. Not because the other nine were bad people — most of us would've done exactly what they did. You get the good news, you feel the relief, and you run straight into your new life without looking back.

I've done it. Maybe you have too — the job came through, the test results were clear, the relationship mended, and somewhere in the rush of relief, the "thank you" just never made it out of your mouth. This isn't a guilt trip. It's an invitation to go back today and finish the prayer you started.

"And one of them, when he saw that he was healed, turned back, and with a loud voice glorified God, and fell down on his face at his feet, giving him thanks." — Luke 17:15-16

🙏 Pray: Lord, bring back to my mind the prayer You already answered, and teach my heart to turn around and thank You before I ask for the next thing. In Jesus' name, amen.
