Ever brace for rejection that never actually shows up? 😔🕊️

Lord, some part of me has been waiting for the door to shut since the day something in me broke. I've rehearsed the rejection so many times I started treating it like a promise You never made.

Thank You for pulling a chair out for me anyway — not after I fix myself, not after I earn it, but now, scars and shame and all. Teach my heart to actually sit down and stay instead of standing by the door waiting to be told to leave.

"Thou shalt eat bread at my table continually." — 2 Samuel 9:7

🙏 Pray: Lord, help me stop bracing for rejection and receive the seat You already set for me. In Jesus' name, amen.

Save this for the day you forget you're still welcome.
