Ever been the one holding someone up because they can't hold themselves up right now? 🤍🙌

Somebody you love can't get to Jesus on their own right now — not because they don't want to, but because the depression, the diagnosis, the addiction, the sheer exhaustion of this season has them flat on their back. You're the one doing the carrying: scheduling the appointments, praying when they can't, showing up when they've stopped asking anyone to.

Lord, I'm tired of carrying someone up a hill they didn't choose to be stuck on. I don't have the strength to fix them and I was never supposed to. All I have is a mat and four corners of faith, so I'm climbing onto the roof and tearing it open until I get them in front of You. Don't let my arms give out before Yours catch them. In Jesus' name, amen.

"When Jesus saw their faith, he said unto the sick of the palsy, Son, thy sins be forgiven thee." — Mark 2:5

🙏 Pray: Lord, let my faith reach the one who's too worn out to believe for themselves right now.
