She stopped bumping into God by accident and reached for Him on purpose 🎬✨

"If I May But Touch," a 4K illustrated short film. A sun-baked crowd presses in around a solitary figure at midday; close in warm firelight as a trembling hand finally reaches for the hem of a robe; then a wide gold dawn burst as she stands healed, arms lifted to the light.

Twelve years of doctors. Twelve years of money spent. Twelve years of the same unanswered ache — and one decision that ended it: stop waiting for the crowd to clear, and reach anyway.

"If I may but touch his clothes, I shall be whole." Mark 5:28
