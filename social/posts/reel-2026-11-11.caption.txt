When the ashes aren't the end of the story 🔥🕊️

🎬 "Pursue, Overtake, Recover All," a 4K illustrated short film. A camp burns to embers at night and a grieving man kneels under it, until a single shaft of light breaks through and he asks God one question. The scene cuts to a fast amber-dusk chase across open wilderness — and lands on a gold sunrise valley where the flock walks back in and nothing is missing.

David didn't lose his family to a story that ends at Ziklag. He lost them to a story that ends with "without fail recover all" — and he had to get up, ask God, and go get it back.

Whatever was taken from you wasn't taken for good. Pursue it.

"Pursue: for thou shalt surely overtake them, and without fail recover all." 1 Samuel 30:8
