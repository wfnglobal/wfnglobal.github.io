The breakthrough that only shows up on try number seven ☁️🏃

A short illustrated film: Elijah bowed low on Mount Carmel, his servant watching a flat empty sea six times in a row, the small hand-shaped cloud that finally rises, and the race to outrun the storm it brings.

Six times the servant came back down with the same report — there is nothing. Elijah never argued with him. He just said go again, like the first six nothings were simply the toll for reaching the seventh look. The cloud that finally rose was only a hand's width wide — small enough to miss if anyone had quit on climb number six.

"And it came to pass at the seventh time, that he said, Behold, there ariseth a little cloud out of the sea, like a man's hand." 1 Kings 18:44
