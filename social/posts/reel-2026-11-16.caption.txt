Three times he asked God to take it away. Three times, the answer was no 🎬🕯️

Some prayers get answered by God removing the thing. Some get answered by something stronger — grace big enough to carry it, every single day it stays.

If there's something in your life that hasn't moved no matter how many times you've asked, this one's for you.

"My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness." — 2 Corinthians 12:9

🙏 Pray: Lord, where You haven't removed it, let Your grace be enough. In Jesus' name, amen.
