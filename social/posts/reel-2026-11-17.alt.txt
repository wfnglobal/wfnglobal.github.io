A 4K vertical illustrated short film, "But God Meant It Unto Good" (Genesis 50:19-21): brothers lower Joseph into a dusty amber-lit cistern at dusk, a single figure sits forgotten in a cold barred cell as bar-shadows mark the passing years, and two silhouettes stand folded into one another in warm rose-gold morning light as the brothers who betrayed him are wept over and forgiven, closing on the Word and Faith Network title card.
