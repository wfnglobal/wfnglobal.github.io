His own brothers sold him. Years later, a prison forgot him too 🎬🕯️

Some betrayals don't come with a quick resolution. They come with years — years in someone else's house, years in a cell nobody remembers you're in, years where it looks like God simply lost track of you.

Joseph's story says otherwise. Every room they put him in, God was already using to put him exactly where he needed to be.

"Ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good." — Genesis 50:20
