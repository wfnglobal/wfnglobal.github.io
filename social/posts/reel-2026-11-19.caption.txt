The enemy saw you go down and started laughing. Watch what Micah does next 🕯️🌅

Some of what knocked you down, you already know, was your own doing. Micah doesn't argue the point — he just refuses to let the enemy have the last word. Rejoice not against me, O mine enemy, he says, because the fall was never going to be the end of the story.

"When I fall, I shall arise; when I sit in darkness, the LORD shall be a light unto me." — Micah 7:8

🙏 Pray: Lord, let my getting back up be louder than anything that watched me fall. In Jesus' name, amen.
