Word and Faith Network illustrated short film "I Cannot Come Down" on Nehemiah 6:2-4: after the WFN intro and title card, a lone silhouetted figure works a torchlit stone wall against a dark amber night sky, then a sealed scroll rests on a flat hazy plain with a small distant figure beckoning from far off, and finally dawn breaks gold as the same figure stands firm atop the finished wall with arms raised, closing on the Word and Faith Network title card.
