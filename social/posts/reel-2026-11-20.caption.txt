The invitation that never looks like an attack — it looks like "just come down for a minute" 🧱🔥

Nehemiah was building a wall with half his city still exposed when the same offer came four separate times: come down, let's talk, let's meet halfway. He never argued with it. He just gave the same answer, every time, until the wall was finished.

🎬 "I Cannot Come Down," a 4K illustrated short film. A figure works a torchlit stone wall by night, a sealed scroll waits on a flat hazy plain with someone far off beckoning across it, and dawn breaks gold over the same figure standing firm atop the finished wall.

"I am doing a great work, so that I cannot come down." — Nehemiah 6:3
