Some seasons the only thing you have left to give is tears. 🌾🔥

This week's film follows a sower who plants in a lean year — the exact seed his family needed for supper, carried into the field while he's still crying over it.

Psalm 126 doesn't let the story end at the weeping. It promises the same hands that planted in tears will come home carrying sheaves, and it uses a word strong enough to settle every doubt: doubtless.

"He that goeth forth and weepeth, bearing precious seed, shall doubtless come again with rejoicing, bringing his sheaves with him." — Psalm 126:6

🙏 Pray: Lord, let every tear I've planted in obedience come back to me as a harvest I can hold in my hands.
