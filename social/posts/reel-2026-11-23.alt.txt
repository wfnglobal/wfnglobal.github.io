4K vertical illustrated short film, "Not As Alone As It Feels." Opens with the WFN global-reach intro and title card, then a weary figure sits alone under a single lamp at midnight in a slate-blue room while the rest of the house sleeps; the scene shifts to a cool teal-indigo dark room where the same figure holds a phone glowing faintly, waiting on a reply that doesn't come; it closes in warm gold-rose morning light as the figure rises with arms lifted, with the spoken verse Isaiah 40:29, "He giveth power to the faint," and a closing title card with the WFN logo and watermark.
