That particular tired that doesn't show up until the house finally goes quiet? 🌙😮‍💨

It's not the kind a nap fixes. It's the kind that waits until everyone else is asleep to ask the real question — does anyone actually see what I'm carrying? The phone stays dark. The reply doesn't come. The silence starts to sound like an answer.

But silence was never the same thing as absence. He giveth power to the faint, and to them that have no might He increaseth strength — not because you finally performed your way back to strong, but because you stayed close enough to receive it.

"He giveth power to the faint; and to them that have no might he increaseth strength." — Isaiah 40:29

🙏 Pray: Lord, when the silence feels like an answer, remind me You were never asleep to what I'm carrying.
