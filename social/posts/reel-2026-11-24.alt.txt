Word and Faith Network illustrated short film "Wilt Thou Be Made Whole?" A weary silhouetted figure waits beside a still teal-grey pool at dawn, then stands in warm gold light breaking through mist beside a robed figure with a staff as the question "do you even want this to change?" appears, then walks forward under an open blue sky carrying a rolled mat, closing on the verse "Rise, take up thy bed, and walk." John 5:8 and the Word and Faith Network title card.
