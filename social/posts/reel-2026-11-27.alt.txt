Vertical illustrated short film from Word & Faith Network, "Thou Shalt Eat Bread at My Table." A lone hooded figure crouches low in a dim, barren hollow at dusk (Lo-debar). A doorway breaks open with gold light and a messenger stands in the seam. The scripture "Fear not: for I will surely shew thee kindness, and thou shalt eat bread at my table continually" appears over a warm golden glow. The film closes on three figures seated together at a banquet table with bread and cups, in deep gold lamplight, closing with the WFN title card.
