🎬 "Thou Shalt Eat Bread at My Table," a 4K illustrated short film. A boy who fell at five grows up hidden in a forgotten village called Lo-debar, is summoned without warning to the king's own house expecting judgment, and finds a permanent chair waiting for him instead.

Mephibosheth spent his whole life bracing to be remembered for the worst thing that ever happened to him. David remembered a promise instead — one made to a father who'd been dead for years — and brought a broken man home to stay.

"Fear not: for I will surely shew thee kindness, and thou shalt eat bread at my table continually." — 2 Samuel 9:7
