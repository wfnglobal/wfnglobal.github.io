🎬 "When Jesus Saw Their Faith," a 4K illustrated short film. Four friends carry a paralyzed man through a house too packed to enter, climb onto the roof, tear it open, and lower him down on his mat until he lands at Jesus' feet.

The paralyzed man never said a word of faith in this story. His friends did the believing for him — refusing a packed house, a hard climb and someone else's roof — and Jesus saw THEIR faith before He ever looked at him.

"When Jesus saw their faith, he said unto the sick of the palsy, Son, thy sins be forgiven thee." — Mark 2:5

🙏 Pray: Lord, give me the kind of faith that carries someone else all the way to You. In Jesus' name, amen.
