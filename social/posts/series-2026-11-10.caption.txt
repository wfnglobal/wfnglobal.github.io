The breakup didn't get the final word. This series does. 💔🕊️

Swipe through for 5 prayers on heartbreak — not the Hallmark kind, the real kind: the relationship you keep replaying, the body that still flinches at their name, the chest that hurts even when your face doesn't show it.

Heartbreak isn't a feeling to just push through until it's gone. It's a wound that needs real tending — in your spirit, in your body, and in a community God never meant you to grieve alone inside of.

01 · The specific grief — not just sad, grieving a whole imagined future (Psalm 147:3)
02 · The decree — releasing every claim they still think they have (Isaiah 43:19)
03 · The body that's still carrying it (Psalm 23:3)
04 · The family that stays when a relationship doesn't (Hebrews 10:24-25)
05 · The Father who holds what they couldn't (Psalm 56:8)
