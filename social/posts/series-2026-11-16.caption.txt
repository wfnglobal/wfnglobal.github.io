The casseroles stopped coming a long time ago. You're still standing in the moment everything changed 🕊️💛

Grief doesn't run on anyone else's calendar — not your family's, not your coworkers', not even your own. It keeps showing up in your body, your sleep, your 3am, long after everyone around you has moved back into their ordinary Tuesdays. This series is for whoever is still carrying something nobody's checking on anymore.

Five prayers, five different voices — reflective, declarative, body-aware, communal, and the voice of a Father who's been watching every tear you thought no one saw.

👉 Swipe slowly. Save this series for the grief you haven't said out loud yet.

01 · Lord, everyone else has moved on. I haven't. (John 11:35)
02 · I decree it today: sorrow is not the last word over my life. (Psalm 30:5)
03 · Notice what grief is doing to your body right now. (Psalm 34:18)
04 · Somebody reading this is grieving alone because nobody knows how to sit with it. (Job 2:13)
05 · Child, I know exactly which tear is which. (Revelation 21:4)
