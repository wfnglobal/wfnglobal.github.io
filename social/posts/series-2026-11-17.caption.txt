The talking got shorter before either of you noticed it happening 🕊️💛

Marriage doesn't usually end in one big explosion. It drifts — a shorter conversation here, an argument that never finished there, a closeness that quietly became roommates sharing a calendar. This series is for the marriage nobody sees the hard parts of, including maybe your own.

Five prayers, five different voices — reflective, declarative, body-aware, communal, and the voice of a Father who was in the room the day you two became one.

👉 Swipe slowly. Save this series for the marriage that needs it more than you've said out loud.

02 · I decree over this marriage: what tries to divide us will not stand. (Amos 3:3)
03 · Notice what the last unfinished argument is still doing to your body. (Ephesians 4:26)
04 · Somebody's marriage is quietly struggling because both of you stopped asking for help. (Ecclesiastes 4:9-10)
05 · Child, I was in the room the day you two became one. (Mark 10:9)
