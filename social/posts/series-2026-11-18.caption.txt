You've read the pamphlet three times and you still don't understand what's happening to your body 🩺🤍

Health has a way of becoming the one thing you can't just push through — the test you're waiting on, the pain with no clear name, the body you used to trust that now feels unfamiliar. This series is for whoever's carrying that tonight, whether it's a diagnosis, a slow recovery, or a fear you haven't said out loud yet.

Five prayers, five different voices — reflective, declarative, body-aware, communal, and the voice of a Father who already knows your chart.

👉 Swipe slowly. Save this series for the body that's still healing.
