Swipe for 5 prayers for the parent who loves too much to stop worrying — not the "just trust God" version, the real kind: the texts you reread for tone, the friend group you can't vet, the kid who's technically fine but you can feel isn't, really.

Lord, I didn't know loving someone could feel this much like holding my breath. I check my phone more than I'd like to admit, replaying our last conversation for the moment I said the wrong thing. Teach me to trade the worry for the prayer, one more time tonight.

"Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it." — Proverbs 22:6

🙏 Pray: Lord, I love this child more than I know how to say — hold what I can't, and be louder than every wrong voice around them. In Jesus' name, amen.

👉 Swipe slowly. Save this series for the parent in your life who's carrying it quietly.

👉 Swipe for all 5:
01 · Lord, I didn't know loving someone could feel this much like holding your breath. (Proverbs 22:6)
02 · I decree over my child: every voice that is not Yours will lose its hold. (Isaiah 54:13)
03 · Notice what the worry over your child is doing to your own shoulders tonight. (Philippians 4:6)
04 · Somebody's child is walking a road tonight that their parent doesn't even know about. (Isaiah 49:25)
05 · Child, I love your child more than you do, and I have never once lost sight of them. (Matthew 18:14)
