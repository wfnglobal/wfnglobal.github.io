The job that takes more than it gives, and the Sunday night dread that starts hours before Monday does 💼😮‍💨

Swipe through five prayers for the work that feels like too much — not to make you quit, but to help you carry it differently. Nehemiah never stopped building his wall even under pressure to come down; these are for the wall you're building too, even when nobody claps for it.

01 · "Better is an handful with quietness, than both the hands full with travail and vexation of spirit." (Ecclesiastes 4:6)
02 · "They shall not labour in vain." (Isaiah 65:23)
03 · "It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows: for so he giveth his beloved sleep." (Psalm 127:2)
04 · "Bear ye one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ." (Galatians 6:2)
05 · "And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men." (Colossians 3:23)
