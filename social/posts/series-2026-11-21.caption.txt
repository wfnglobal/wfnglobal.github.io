The box from your old life is still half-packed, and the new one doesn't feel like yours yet 📦🕯️

Starting over doesn't always come from a bad decision — sometimes it's a layoff, a move, a breakup, a diagnosis, or a season that just ended without checking with you first. Swipe for five prayers for the wobbly first weeks of a chapter you never rehearsed for.

Lord, I didn't choose this beginning, but here I am in it — tired, a little unsteady, still finding where things go. Meet me in the unpacking, in Jesus' name, amen.

"Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it?" — Isaiah 43:19

🙏 Pray: Lord, give me grace for a beginning I never asked for, and the strength to stop measuring it against the one I planned. In Jesus' name, amen.

👉 Swipe for all 5:
01 · Lord, I didn't choose this chapter, but here I am starting it anyway. (Isaiah 43:19)
02 · I decree: this new beginning is not a demotion — it is a door. (2 Corinthians 5:17)
03 · Notice how tired starting over makes your whole body feel before you've even started. (Isaiah 40:29)
04 · Somebody in this family is quietly starting over and hoping nobody asks too many questions. (Hebrews 10:24)
05 · Child, I was already in the new place before you ever packed a box. (Jeremiah 29:11)

👉 Swipe slowly. Save this for the chapter you didn't plan to start.
