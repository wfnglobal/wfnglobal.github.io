Waiting is one of the heaviest things a person can carry quietly — for the test results, the job offer, the apology, the child to come home, the prayer you've prayed so many times you've lost count. Nobody throws you a party for holding on.

So here are five prayers for the waiting season, five different voices for five different days — because some days you need to reflect, some days you need to decree, and some days you just need Someone to say they see you.

01 · "They that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles." (Isaiah 40:31)
02 · "Though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry." (Habakkuk 2:3)
03 · "Be still, and know that I am God." (Psalm 46:10)
04 · "Bear ye one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ." (Galatians 6:2)
05 · "For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end." (Jeremiah 29:11)
