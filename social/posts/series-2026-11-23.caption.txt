Running on fumes and calling it faithfulness? 🪫🕯️

Burnout doesn't always look dramatic. Sometimes it's just saying yes one more time when your body already said no, or waking up tired after a full night's sleep and not telling anyone. Nobody throws a party for the person still showing up on empty.

So here are five prayers for the tank that's been on empty longer than you've let on, five different voices for five different days - because some days you need to reflect honestly, some days you need to decree out loud, and some days you just need Someone to gather you like a tired child.

01 · "Come ye yourselves apart into a desert place, and rest a while." (Mark 6:31)
02 · "My yoke is easy, and my burden is light." (Matthew 11:30)
03 · "For I have satiated the weary soul, and I have replenished every sorrowful soul." (Jeremiah 31:25)
04 · "Wherefore comfort yourselves together, and edify one another." (1 Thessalonians 5:11)
05 · "As one whom his mother comforteth, so will I comfort you." (Isaiah 66:13)
