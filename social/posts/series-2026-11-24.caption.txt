The one everyone calls for updates, but nobody calls just to ask how they're doing 🤍🩺

Swipe for 5 prayers on caregiving — not the "you're so strong" kind, the real kind: the back that aches from lifting, the sleep you haven't had in weeks, the guilt for feeling tired of loving someone this much. Whether it's a parent, a spouse, or a child you're caring for, this is for the one holding everyone else together.

01 · Lord, I love them, and I'm exhausted — both are true at once (Psalm 61:2)
02 · I decree: I will not run out before this season does (Isaiah 40:29)
03 · Lord, my body has been running on adrenaline longer than it should (Psalm 73:26)
04 · We see the one everyone calls for updates but nobody calls just to check on (Galatians 6:2)
05 · Child, who has been taking care of you while you take care of them? (Isaiah 46:4)
