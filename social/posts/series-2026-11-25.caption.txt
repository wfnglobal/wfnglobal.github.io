Everyone at the table has a ring story except you 💍🕊️

Singleness doesn't usually get its own sermon — it gets mentioned in passing, like a waiting room everyone's trying to leave. This series is for the season nobody prepared you for: the holiday questions, the group chat full of engagement photos, the quiet feeling of being the only one whose life "hasn't started yet."

Five prayers, five different voices — reflective, declarative, body-aware, communal, and the voice of a Father who isn't withholding anything from you.

"For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end." — Jeremiah 29:11

🙏 Pray: Lord, teach me to live fully in this season instead of waiting to start living in the next one. In Jesus' name, amen.

👉 Swipe slowly. Save this for the single season you've stopped explaining to people.

01 · Lord, I'm not where I pictured I'd be by now, and I feel it every time an invitation says plus-one. (Ecclesiastes 3:1)
02 · I decree: I am not incomplete because I am unmarried. (Isaiah 54:5)
03 · Notice what another engagement post just did to your shoulders. (Psalm 139:14)
04 · Somebody at church stopped coming because every sermon illustration assumed they were married. (Romans 12:4-5)
05 · Child, I am not withholding — I am building something you can't see yet. (Jeremiah 29:11)
