You promised yourself last time was really the last time 😔🔁

Nobody talks about this one out loud — the thing you keep swearing off and keep going back to, whether it's a screen, a bottle, a substance, a person, or a habit that feels bigger than your willpower. This series isn't about shaming you into trying harder. It's for the real, exhausting, two-steps-forward-one-step-back fight that most of us are either in or hiding from somebody who loves us.

Five prayers, five different voices — reflective, declarative, body-aware, communal, and the voice of a Father whose mercy doesn't run out overnight.

"It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness." — Lamentations 3:22-23

🙏 Pray: Lord, meet me in the pattern I can't seem to break, and give me mercy for this morning, not just forgiveness for last night. In Jesus' name, amen.

👉 Swipe slowly. Save this for the habit you've stopped telling people about.

01 · I hate what I keep doing, and I still keep doing it. (Romans 7:15)
02 · I decree: this stronghold does not get to keep my name. (2 Corinthians 10:4)
03 · Notice what happens in your chest right before you reach for it again. (Psalm 51:10)
04 · Somebody in this church is hiding a relapse they're terrified to say out loud. (Galatians 6:1-2)
05 · Child, this morning is not a continuation of last night's failure. (Lamentations 3:22-23)
