Today's sermon: "The Lawful Captive Shall Go Free" (Isaiah 49:24-25)

Some captivity looks legal. You signed for it, you walked into it, and the enemy has the paperwork. Maybe it's a debt, an addiction, a relationship, a habit you said yes to a hundred times.

Here's the good news: God didn't argue about whose fault it was. He answered with a promise. The captor may have a claim, but the blood of Jesus outranks every signature you ever made in the dark.

"But thus saith the LORD, Even the captives of the mighty shall be taken away, and the prey of the terrible shall be delivered: for I will contend with him that contendeth with thee, and I will save thy children." (Isaiah 49:25, KJV)

✨ Inside the full teaching:
• Why "lawful" captivity is still not permanent captivity
• "Graven upon the palms of my hands": how God remembers you
• What it means that God fights for your children too
