Today's sermon: "Possess Your Possessions" (Obadiah 1:17-18)

Deliverance gets you out. Holiness keeps you from going back. But possession — actually walking onto the land God already signed over to you — is the part most of us skip, because it means treating the old defeat like kindling instead of a threat.

"And the house of Jacob shall be a fire, and the house of Esau for stubble, and they shall kindle in them, and devour them." Obadiah 1:18

✨ Inside the full teaching:
• Why the same God who delivered you also armed you
• What it actually means to "possess" instead of just survive
• The difference between holding a deed and walking the land

What's the "stubble" in your life that's been treated like a threat for too long? Say it below. 👇
