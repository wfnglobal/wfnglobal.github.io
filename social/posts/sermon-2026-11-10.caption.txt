When the breakthrough has been one touch away for twelve years 🩸✨

Today's sermon: "If I May But Touch" (Mark 5:27-29)

Twelve years of doctors, twelve years of shame, and a whole crowd standing between her and Jesus. She didn't wait for the crowd to clear, for an invitation, or for someone to walk her up front. She had one plan left: press in, reach for the hem, and let faith do what twelve years of trying never could.

Here's what wrecks me about this story: dozens of people were touching Jesus that day by accident, in the crush of the crowd, and nothing happened for any of them. Only one touch — hers — carried faith instead of friction. The same power you've been bumping into in church, in scripture, in every ordinary Sunday, is still available the second you stop colliding with God and actually reach for Him.

"For she said, If I may but touch his garment, I shall be whole. And straightway the fountain of her blood was dried up; and she felt in her body that she was healed of that plague." Mark 5:28-29

• What "straightway" means for the thing you've been waiting years on
