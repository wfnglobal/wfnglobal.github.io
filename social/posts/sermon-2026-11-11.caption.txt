Today's sermon: "Pursue, Overtake, Recover All" (1 Samuel 30:6, 8)

Ziklag was ashes before David even got close enough to call out any names — his wives gone, his sons gone, and the six hundred men who'd just lost everything too turning on the one man who'd led them through everything else. That's the moment most of us quit in: devastated, surrounded, and suddenly alone in a crowd.

David didn't defend himself or wait for the grief talk to settle down. He encouraged himself in the Lord, then asked the only question that mattered — and heaven didn't just say yes. It said pursue, for thou shalt surely overtake them, and without fail recover all.

"And David enquired at the LORD, saying, Shall I pursue after this troop? shall I overtake them? And he answered him, Pursue: for thou shalt surely overtake them, and without fail recover all." (1 Samuel 30:8, KJV)

✨ Inside the full teaching:
• Why David went to God before he went to his men
• What "without fail recover all" promises that "eventually, some of it" doesn't
• How Ziklag becomes the start of David's comeback, not the end of his story
