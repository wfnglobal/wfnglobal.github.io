Today's sermon: "Trade Your Yoke" (Matthew 11:28-30)

He never told the weary to stop carrying everything, and He never waited for you to get your life together before He extended the invitation. He said come — heavy laden, unfinished, exactly as you are — and trade.

Rest, in this verse, was never going to mean carrying nothing. It means carrying the right thing, at the right pace, next to Him instead of alone.

"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light." Matthew 11:28-30

✨ Inside the full teaching:
• Why "take my yoke" is an invitation, not another burden
• What it means that His yoke is "easy" — fitted to you, not generic
• How to actually trade your yoke instead of just stacking His on top
