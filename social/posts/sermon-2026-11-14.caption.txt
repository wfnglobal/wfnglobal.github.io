Today's sermon: "What Is That in Thine Hand?" (Exodus 4:2-4)

Moses had a whole speech prepared about why he wasn't the man for the job — not eloquent, not confident, not convinced anyone would listen. God skipped past every excuse and asked one question instead: what is that in thine hand?

It was a stick. The same stick he'd carried through forty forgettable years in the wilderness. But the moment he threw it down in obedience instead of holding onto it in fear, it became the rod of God — the very instrument that would part a sea and humble a nation.

"And the LORD said unto him, What is that in thine hand? And he said, A rod." Exodus 4:2 (KJV)

✨ Inside the full teaching:
• Why God never argued with a single one of Moses' excuses
• What actually happens the moment the rod hits the ground
• How the ordinary thing in your hand becomes the proof of who sent you
