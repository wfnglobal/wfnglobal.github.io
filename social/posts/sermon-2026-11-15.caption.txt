Today's sermon: "Can These Bones Live?" (Ezekiel 37:3-5)

God didn't ask Ezekiel to fix the valley. He asked him one question with no safe answer — can these bones live? — and Ezekiel gave the only honest reply left: Lord, only You know. Then God handed him the assignment nobody expects: don't wait for the breath, preach to the bones first.

That's still how it works. Whatever's gone quiet and dry in your life doesn't need you to keep diagnosing it — it needs you to open your mouth and say what God already said over it, until dead starts rattling back toward alive.

"And I prophesied as he commanded me, and the breath came into them, and they lived, and stood up upon their feet, an exceeding great army." Ezekiel 37:10

✨ Inside the full teaching:
• Why "thou knowest" is the most honest prayer in the Bible
• What it means that God told the prophet to preach, not just wait
• How a valley of bones became an exceeding great army
