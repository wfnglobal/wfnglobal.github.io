Three prayers. Same answer: no. Here's why that wasn't heaven going silent 📖🔥

Paul had a thorn in his flesh — we don't actually know what it was, and maybe that's on purpose, so every reader can put their own thorn there. He asked God to remove it. Once. Twice. A third time. And God's answer wasn't the one he was praying for.

Heaven didn't say no because Paul lacked faith. Heaven said, My grace is sufficient — because the very thing he wanted gone was about to become the place where God's power showed up the loudest. Some prayers get answered by removing the thorn. Some get answered by something stronger: grace big enough to carry it, every single day it stays.

"My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness." — 2 Corinthians 12:9

🙏 Pray: Lord, where You haven't removed it, make Your grace enough. In Jesus' name, amen.
