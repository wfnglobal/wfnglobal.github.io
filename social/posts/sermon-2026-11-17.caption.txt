Thirteen years between the pit and the palace. Here's what filled the gap 📖✨

Joseph's brothers sold him into slavery, lied to their father about it, and moved on with their lives. He spent years in another man's house, then more years forgotten in a prison cell he didn't deserve.

When the story finally turns, he doesn't get even with the brothers who ruined his life — he weeps over them, and says the sentence that ends the whole cycle: ye thought evil against me, but God meant it unto good. The pit, the prison, the years nobody explained — all of it had already been rewritten before he even knew it was happening.

"Ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good." — Genesis 50:20
