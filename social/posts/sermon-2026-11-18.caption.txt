All night, every skill he had, and nothing to show for it. Then one word changed everything 📖🎣

Peter had fished that lake since he was a boy — he knew the currents, the depths, the right hour to cast. He'd still worked the whole night and come up empty. When Jesus finished teaching the crowd from his boat, He didn't tell Peter to go home and rest. He told him to launch out into the deep and try again, in broad daylight, with the one crowd that had just watched him fail.

Peter could have explained why it wouldn't work — he was the expert, after all. Instead he said the sentence that's the whole sermon: nevertheless at thy word, I will let down the net. The net came up so full it started to tear, and he had to call the other boat over before both of them began to sink under the catch.

"Nevertheless at thy word I will let down the net." — Luke 5:5

🙏 Pray: Lord, let me obey what You're saying even when my own experience argues against it. In Jesus' name, amen.
