The enemy watched you fall and started laughing. Watch what happens next 👊🔥

Micah doesn't pretend his fall was innocent. He says it outright — I have sinned against him — no spin, no blaming the circumstances. But in the same breath he says something the enemy wasn't ready for: when I fall, I shall arise.

That's the whole sermon. Not "I never fall." Not "it wasn't my fault." Just — I get back up, because the Lord is my light even in the dark I walked myself into. Whatever knocked you down today, your story doesn't end on the floor.

"When I fall, I shall arise; when I sit in darkness, the LORD shall be a light unto me." — Micah 7:8

🙏 Pray: Lord, when I fall, don't let me stay there — be my light until I'm back on my feet. In Jesus' name, amen.
