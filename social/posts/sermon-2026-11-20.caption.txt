The invitation always sounds reasonable. That's exactly how you know to say no 🧱🔥

Nehemiah wasn't hiding from a fight. He was building a wall with weapons close by, half the city still exposed, and right in the middle of that urgent work a letter arrives: come down, let's meet, let's talk it over — friendly, reasonable, four separate times.

He never took the bait. He didn't argue with the invitation or explain himself twice. He just kept giving the same answer: "I am doing a great work, so that I cannot come down." The thing that couldn't stop him by attack tried to stop him by appointment instead — and it still didn't work.

"I am doing a great work, so that I cannot come down." — Nehemiah 6:3

🙏 Pray: Lord, when what couldn't stop me by attack tries to stop me by appointment, give me Nehemiah's same answer, every time. In Jesus' name, amen.
