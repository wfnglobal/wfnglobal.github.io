Your eyes can be wide open and still only showing you half of what's actually there 👁️🔥

The servant looked outside at dawn, saw an entire army surrounding the city, and ran back in convinced it was over. He wasn't imagining it — the danger was real. What he hadn't seen yet was the bigger truth standing right beside it.

Elisha didn't argue with what the servant saw. He prayed one short sentence — LORD, open his eyes — and the same hillside that looked like certain defeat turned out to be full of horses and chariots of fire. Nothing outside that city changed. What changed was what the servant could finally see.

"Fear not: for they that be with us are more than they that be with them." — 2 Kings 6:16

🙏 Pray: Lord, before I believe everything I'm seeing, open my eyes to what's standing with me. In Jesus' name, amen.
