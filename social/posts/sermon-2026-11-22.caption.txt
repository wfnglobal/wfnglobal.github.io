Ever plant something expensive in a season you couldn't afford to? 🌾😢

In a lean year, a farmer stood over the exact seed his children were counting on for supper — and the Bible doesn't pretend he wasn't crying when he carried it into the field. Real sacrifice usually costs exactly what you were saving for something else.

But Psalm 126 doesn't end at the weeping. It ends at a word that cancels doubt completely: doubtless. He shall doubtless come again with rejoicing, bringing his sheaves with him. Not maybe. Not if the weather holds. Doubtless.

"He that goeth forth and weepeth, bearing precious seed, shall doubtless come again with rejoicing, bringing his sheaves with him." — Psalm 126:6

🙏 Pray: Lord, let every tear I've planted in obedience come back to me as a harvest I can hold in my hands.

✨ Inside the full teaching:
— Why God lets the sacrifice cost exactly what you can't spare
— The one word, "doubtless," that cancels every maybe
— How to keep sowing when the field hasn't answered yet
