Ever feel like God is sleeping through the exact thing that's sinking you? ⛵😟

The storm on the Sea of Galilee was real and violent — waves breaking over the sides, water filling the boat. The disciples had every reason to panic. What broke something in them wasn't just the wind; it was looking back and seeing Jesus asleep on a pillow like none of it was His problem.

So they woke Him with the question every frightened heart eventually asks: Master, carest thou not that we perish? He didn't explain Himself. He didn't apologize for resting. He stood up and spoke three words to the wind and the sea, and the same storm that terrified grown fishermen simply stopped.

"Peace, be still." — Mark 4:39

🙏 Pray: Lord, even when it looks like You're asleep to what's drowning me, let me remember You're one word away from calming it completely.
