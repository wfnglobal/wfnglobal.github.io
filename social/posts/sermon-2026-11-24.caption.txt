What if the thing keeping you stuck isn't your circumstance — it's that you quietly stopped expecting anything else? 🩺🕊️

A man lay by the pool of Bethesda for thirty-eight years. Crowds of sick folks waited with him, and the custom was that whoever stepped into the water first, when it stirred, got healed. He never got there first. Not once. Thirty-eight years of almost.

Jesus walked up and asked the strangest question: wilt thou be made whole? Not "do you want to try the water again." Not "do you want sympathy." He asked if the man actually wanted to be whole — because the longer we carry something, the more our whole life gets built around carrying it. Then, without ever touching the pool, Jesus said three things: rise, take up thy bed, and walk.

"Rise, take up thy bed, and walk." — John 5:8

🙏 Pray: Lord, I don't need another almost — I need You to speak and change what nothing else has reached.
