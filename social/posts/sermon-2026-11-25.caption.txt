What do you do when you're not sure God will rescue you — but you still won't back down? 🔥🙌

Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego stood in front of a furnace and a king who could kill them, and they said something most of us are scared to say out loud: even if God doesn't come through the way we're hoping, we still won't bow.

That's not weak faith. That's the strongest kind — the kind that doesn't need a guaranteed happy ending to keep standing. He was with them before the fire ever got hot, and He's with you in whatever you're walking into today.

"But if not, be it known unto thee, O king, that we will not serve thy gods, nor worship the golden image which thou hast set up." — Daniel 3:18

🙏 Pray: Lord, give me a faith that stands even when I don't know how this ends. In Jesus' name, amen.
