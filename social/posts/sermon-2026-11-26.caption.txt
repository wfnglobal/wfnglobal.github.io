Why does it sting so much when someone forgets to say thank you? Because Jesus felt it too 🙏✨

Ten lepers stood at a distance and cried out, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us." He didn't touch them, didn't pray a long prayer over them — He just told them to go show themselves to the priest, and on the way, all ten were healed. That's the easy part of the story. Here's the part that still stings: only one of them, a Samaritan, came back.

Jesus didn't ask the question to shame anyone. He asked it because gratitude is where relationship actually grows. The other nine got their healing and moved on with their lives. The one who turned back got something more — Jesus told him his faith had made him whole, a wholeness the other nine never came back for.

"And Jesus answering said, Were there not ten cleansed? but where are the nine?" — Luke 17:17

🙏 Pray: Lord, make me the one who turns back, not just the one who receives. In Jesus' name, amen.
