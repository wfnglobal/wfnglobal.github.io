He thought the knock at the door would be a sword 🗡️🤍

Mephibosheth was five years old when his nurse fled with him in her arms and dropped him running. Crippled in both feet, he grew up hidden in a place called Lo-debar — a name that literally means no pasture, nothing growing there at all.

Years later, a summons came from the king's own house. He assumed it was finally the sentence he'd been bracing for his whole life — the old king's family, wiped out the way these stories usually go. Instead David asked one question: is there anyone left of Saul's house I can show kindness to? And he found a man who still called himself a dead dog.

"Thou shalt eat bread at my table continually." — 2 Samuel 9:7

🙏 Pray: Lord, let me stop expecting the sword and start believing the kindness. In Jesus' name, amen.
