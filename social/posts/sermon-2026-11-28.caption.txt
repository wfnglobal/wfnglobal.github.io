Four friends couldn't wait for the line to move 🏠🙌

The house was so packed nobody could get near the door, so four men carried their paralyzed friend onto the roof, pulled it apart, and lowered him down on his mat until he landed right at Jesus' feet.

Mark doesn't say the paralyzed man had faith. He says Jesus saw THEIR faith — the friends who refused to let a packed house or a broken roof stop them — and that's the faith that got their friend healed and forgiven in the same breath.

"When Jesus saw their faith, he said unto the sick of the palsy, Son, thy sins be forgiven thee." — Mark 2:5

🙏 Pray: Lord, give me the kind of faith that carries someone else all the way to You. In Jesus' name, amen.

— Why Mark names their faith, not his
