targeted against dreams of backwardness and stagnation. Each one is woven with relevant Bible verses to make them prophetic, declarative, and rooted in God’s Word. Pray them fervently in faith, preferably aloud, and personalize them as the Holy Spirit leads.
1.  Heavenly Father, Your Word declares in Deuteronomy 1:6-7 that “The LORD our God spake unto us in Horeb, saying, Ye have dwelt long enough in this mount: Turn you, and take your journey…” I decree that every dream of dwelling in old places, former houses, villages, or schools—symbols of backwardness—is canceled now. I arise and take my journey forward in Jesus’ name!
2.  O Lord, according to Jeremiah 29:11, “For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end.” Every dream of stagnation designed to derail Your good plans for my life is destroyed by fire, and I receive divine acceleration in Jesus’ name.
3.  By the authority in Proverbs 4:18—“But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day”—I declare that my path shines brighter and brighter. Every dream pulling me backward is turned to confusion, and I move forward gloriously in Jesus’ name.
4.  Father, Your Word in Isaiah 43:18-19 says, “Remember ye not the former things, neither consider the things of old. Behold, I will do a new thing…” Every dream of former things, old cycles, or retrogression is nullified. I step into the new thing You are doing in my life now, in Jesus’ name!
5.  In the name of Jesus, I break every yoke of backwardness as declared in Isaiah 10:27—“And it shall come to pass in that day, that his burden shall be taken away from off thy shoulder, and his yoke from off thy neck, and the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing.” The anointing destroys every yoke of stagnation from dreams, in Jesus’ name.
6.  Lord, Philippians 3:13-14 declares, “Brethren, I count not myself to have apprehended: but this one thing I do, forgetting those things which are behind, and reaching forth unto those things which are before, I press toward the mark…” Every dream forcing me to look back or return to the past is rejected; I press forward to the prize in Jesus’ name.
7.  Every satanic injection of backwardness through dreams is scattered, according to Nahum 1:13—“For now will I break his yoke from off thee, and will burst thy bonds in sunder.” I am loosed from every chain of stagnation, in the mighty name of Jesus.
8.  Father, as in Habakkuk 2:3—“For the vision is yet for an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come…”—every dream causing delay and stagnation is overturned. My vision speaks and manifests speedily, in Jesus’ name.
9.  I cancel every evil dream of retrogression, for Revelation 3:8 says, “I know thy works: behold, I have set before thee an open door, and no man can shut it…” Every closed door from backward dreams is opened, and no power can shut what You have opened, in Jesus’ name.
10.  O God, arise and scatter every power assigned to make me stagnant, as in Psalm 68:1—“Let God arise, let his enemies be scattered: let them also that hate him flee before him.” Backwardness flees from my life now, in Jesus’ name!
11.  By the power in Galatians 3:13—“Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us…”—every ancestral curse of backwardness manifesting in dreams is broken and nullified over my destiny, in Jesus’ name.
12.  Lord, Your Word in Micah 7:8 declares, “Rejoice not against me, O mine enemy: when I fall, I shall arise; when I sit in darkness, the LORD shall be a light unto me.” Every dream of falling back or sitting in stagnation is reversed—I arise and shine, in Jesus’ name!
13.  Every spirit of delay and backwardness from dreams is consumed by fire, according to Zechariah 1:21—“Then said I, What come these to do? And he spake, saying, These are the horns which have scattered Judah… but these are come to fray them, to cast out the horns of the Gentiles…” Every horn of backwardness is cast out, in Jesus’ name.
14.  Father, as 1 Peter 5:10 promises, “But the God of all grace, who hath called us unto his eternal glory by Christ Jesus, after that ye have suffered a while, make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle you”—after every attack of stagnation in dreams, You establish and settle me in progress, in Jesus’ name.
15.  I reject and destroy every garment of backwardness worn in dreams, for Isaiah 61:3 says You give “beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness…” I receive the garment of praise and forward movement, in Jesus’ name.
