=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; around midday) ===
Detained in the Village

Twenty years.

That's how long he lived in New York.

Twenty winters of early trains and double shifts. Money sent home every month.

He watched people who arrived after him buy houses, open businesses and move on.

He wasn't lazy. He wasn't foolish.

Every door opened just wide enough for him to see what he couldn't have.

Then a man of God prayed with him and said something he never expected:

"You're being held back in your village."

His body was in America. His life had never left.

The pastor didn't just pray. He bought a ticket and flew with him to Nigeria.

At midnight in a village hotel, the pastor shook him awake:

"Get up. Someone is calling your spirit tonight."

They prayed until daybreak.

What happened that morning changed his life, and gave this book its title…
