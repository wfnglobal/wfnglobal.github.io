=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; around midday) ===
Pursue. Overtake. Recover All.

David came home to smoke.

Three days on the road. Six hundred tired men. And Ziklag was gone — burned with fire.

Every wife. Every son. Every daughter. Carried off.

They wept until they had no strength left to weep.

Then the grief turned.

Somebody picked up a stone. Then another. The men who had followed David through caves and deserts started talking about killing him.

Loss behind him. Stones in front of him. No prophet. No friend. Nobody coming.

That's the moment most of us quit.

David did something else — four words that flipped the whole chapter. And then a second thing almost nobody notices, before he ever lifted a sword…
