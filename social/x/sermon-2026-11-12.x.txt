=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; around midday) ===
Go Again Seven Times

Three and a half years. Not one drop of rain.

The brooks were dust. The fields were cracked. The cattle were dying.

Then fire fell on Mount Carmel — and the whole nation hit the ground.

Everybody went down the mountain to celebrate.

Elijah went up the mountain to pray.

Because the fire had fallen… but the rain still hadn't.

He put his face between his knees and sent his servant to look at the sea.

The servant came back with two words: "There is nothing."

Blue sky. Dry wind. Same as yesterday.

Maybe that's your report right now. You prayed. You believed. You looked toward the sea — and nothing.

Here's what most people never notice: Elijah didn't argue with the report. He didn't explain the delay.

He said three words that have kept believers on their knees for three thousand years…
