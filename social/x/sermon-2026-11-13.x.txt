=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; around midday) ===
Trade Your Yoke

Some tiredness a nap can't touch.

It lives in your chest. Behind your eyes. At the back of your mind at 3 AM.

You've carried it so long, and so well, that nobody around you knows how heavy it is.

Jesus knows.

And to people exactly like you, He stood up in a crowd and said one word before anything else: "Come."

Not "get yourself together, then come."

Just — come. If you're tired, you qualify. That's the whole entry requirement.

But here's the part almost everyone reads past.

He doesn't offer the weary man a life with nothing on his shoulders.

He offers him… another yoke.

Wait — the exhausted get handed something else to carry?

Yes. And once you see why, you'll never read "my yoke is easy" the same way again…
