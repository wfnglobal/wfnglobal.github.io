=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; around midday) ===
What Is That in Thine Hand?

Too old. Too broke. Too late. Not gifted enough.

You have a list. Everybody does.

Moses had one too — and his was airtight.

Eighty years old. A fugitive with a dead Egyptian in his past. Slow of speech.

Forty years in the backside of the desert, keeping sheep that weren't even his.

So when God said "Go to Pharaoh," Moses read Him the whole list.

God didn't argue with a single line.

He pointed at the plainest thing Moses owned — a worn shepherd's stick — and asked one question.

Then He told him to throw it down.

And what hit the sand was the very thing Moses had been running from his whole life…
