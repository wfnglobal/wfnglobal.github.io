=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; around midday) ===
Can These Bones Live?

There's a valley in your life you don't visit anymore.

The marriage that went cold. The dream that dried out. The child who walked away. The calling you buried under bills and years.

You still believe in God.

You just stopped believing in that.

Ezekiel knew a valley like that.

God set him down in the middle of it — and it was full. Bones everywhere. Not freshly fallen. Very dry. Dead so long it didn't even look like a tragedy anymore. Just scenery.

Then God asked the strangest question in the whole book: "Son of man, can these bones live?"

Every fact in that valley said no.

Ezekiel didn't say yes. But he refused to say no.

And what God told him to do next sounds ridiculous — until you see what happened when he obeyed…
