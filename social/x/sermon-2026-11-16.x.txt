=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; around midday) ===
When the Thorn Stays

There's a prayer you know by heart.

Take it away, Lord. Heal it. Lift it. Fix it.

You fasted over it. You stood in the prayer line. You cried about it in the car where nobody could hear.

And this morning, it was still there.

If that's you, you're in good company.

Paul — the man who saw the third heaven, whose handkerchiefs healed the sick, who shook a viper off into the fire — had one too.

He called it a thorn in the flesh.

He prayed three times for it to go.

It stayed.

But heaven didn't go quiet on him. God answered — just not the way Paul asked.

And the answer He gave changed the way Paul saw every weak place in his life…
