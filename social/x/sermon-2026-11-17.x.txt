=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; around midday) ===
But God Meant It Unto Good

He was seventeen.

A dream. A coat. A father who loved him out loud.

That was enough for his brothers to hate him.

Far from home, they saw him coming. They stripped off the coat and threw him into a dry pit.

Then they sat down and ate lunch.

While their brother cried in a hole.

Then they sold him. Twenty pieces of silver.

Maybe your pit had a different name. A friend who sold you out. A family that talked about you instead of to you. Someone you trusted with your heart.

Joseph's story got worse before it got better — a lie that sent him to prison, and a man who promised to remember him and forgot.

Then, years later, those same brothers were on their faces before him.

He had the throne. The guards. The power.

One word and it was over.

What he said next is the sentence that heals families…
