=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; around midday) ===
Nevertheless at Thy Word

Some of you worked harder this year than ever.

You prayed. You planned. You showed up early and stayed late.

And you came home with nothing.

Peter knew that feeling. He wasn't a beginner — he was a professional fisherman. He knew the lake, the weather, the best hours.

He fished all night.

Not a single fish.

So he was on the shore, washing empty nets, ready to go home and sleep it off.

Then Jesus borrowed his boat. Preached a sermon from it. And when He finished, He gave the tired professional an order that made no sense:

"Launch out into the deep, and let down your nets."

Broad daylight. Wrong time. Wrong place. A carpenter telling a fisherman how to fish.

Peter had every reason to say no.

What he said instead is one word — and it's the word that changes the night you just had…
