=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; around midday) ===
When I Fall, I Shall Arise

Not every fall happens in private.

Some of us went down where everyone could see.

A marriage. A business. A habit we swore was gone. A promise we broke.

And the hardest part wasn't hitting the ground.

It was the faces.

The ones who smiled a little. The ones who said, "Where is your God now?"

The prophet Micah knew that sound. His nation was collapsing. And he was honest — some of it was their own fault.

He didn't make excuses.

But he also didn't stay down.

He turned toward the enemy who was enjoying the show and said one sentence every fallen person needs to learn by heart:

"Rejoice not against me, O mine enemy."

Then he told that enemy exactly why the celebration was premature…
