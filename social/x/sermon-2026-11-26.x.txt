=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; around midday) ===
Where Are the Nine?

Ten men. One disease. Zero options.

Leprosy didn't just eat your skin.
It ate your name, your house, your seat at the family table.

So they stood at a distance — the law said they had to — and shouted the only prayer they had left:
"Jesus, Master, have mercy on us."

He didn't touch them.
Didn't pray over them.
He just said, "Go show yourselves to the priests."

Problem: they were still lepers.
Same spots. Same skin.

They went anyway.
And somewhere on that road, ten men looked down and saw brand-new skin.

Then the story splits in two.
Nine keep walking.
One stops in the middle of the road and turns around.

And what Jesus says when He sees only one of them coming back is a question nobody in that crowd could answer…

=== D) STORY POST (X Premium long-form; stands alone — early afternoon or evening) ===
It's Thursday, November 26.

Somewhere today, a family is sitting around a table, and somebody's uncle is making everyone say one thing they're thankful for.

And half the table is panicking.

Because if we're honest, we're not ungrateful.
We're just… busy.

The job you fasted for is now the job you complain about.
The baby you begged God for is now "the kids, again."
The apartment you prayed over is now just where the bills arrive.
The diagnosis that came back clear in March? You haven't mentioned it since April.

You didn't forget God.
You just moved on faster than your thank-you did.

And here's the quiet lie that sneaks in after a blessing:
"That's just how things worked out."

No. Somebody worked it out.

I want to take you to a dusty road between Samaria and Galilee.

Ten men with leprosy. Standing far off, because the law said they had to.
No hugs. No family dinners. No seat at anybody's table.

They shout, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us."
And Jesus says, "Go show yourselves to the priests."

They still have the spots. They go anyway.
And on the way — new skin. All ten.

Nine keep walking. Places to be. Paperwork to file.
One stops. Turns around. Runs back. Falls on his face at Jesus' feet, shouting glory at the top of his lungs.

Just like you, the nine weren't bad people.
They were obeying. They were literally on their way to church.

And Jesus asks: "Were there not ten cleansed? But where are the nine?"

Write these down:

Heaven can count. Jesus knew the number He healed and the number who came back.

You can follow every instruction and still forget the Instructor.

Nine got their skin back. One got his whole life back.

So today:
Before you ask God for anything, go back.
Name one thing out loud. Specifically.
Then thank Him for the Healer, not just the healing.

Say it like you mean it:
"Lord, I am not one of the nine. I see what You did, and I came back to give You glory."

Turn around.
The miracle was on the road. Wholeness is at His feet.

Happy Thanksgiving to my American family.

In Jesus' name.
