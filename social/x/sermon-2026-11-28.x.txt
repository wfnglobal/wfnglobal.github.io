=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; around midday) ===
When Jesus Saw Their Faith

He couldn't walk.
He couldn't push.
He couldn't even get himself to the door.

So four friends picked up the corners of his mat and carried him across Capernaum.

Word was out: Jesus was in the house.
By the time they got there, the house was packed.
Not one inch of space. Not even at the door.

Shoulders. Elbows. Backs turned.
The crowd that came for Jesus was the very thing standing between a broken man and Jesus.

Most of us would have set the mat down right there.
Said a quick prayer over it.
"We tried. We'll come back next week."

These four looked at the door.
Then they looked up.

And what they did on that roof made Jesus say the one thing nobody in that room expected to hear…

=== D) STORY POST (X Premium long-form; stands alone — early afternoon or evening) ===
There's a name in your phone you haven't texted in a while.

Not because you stopped caring.
Because you got tired.

You prayed for them for years.
You drove them to rehab. Twice.
You sat up with them at 2 a.m.
You invited them to church so many times it got awkward.
You covered their rent. You covered for them.

And somewhere along the way, your arms just… gave out.

So you set them down.
Quietly. Politely. No announcement.

And the lie moved right in:
"Some people just don't change. I've done all I can."

I want to take you to a house in Capernaum.

Four men. One mat. One friend on it who can't move a muscle.
They carry him across town because they heard Jesus is in the house.

They get there and — nothing. No room. Not even at the door.
Just like you, they did everything right and walked straight into a wall of people.

So they climb.
Up the outside stairs. Onto the flat roof.
And they start digging.

Picture it. Jesus is mid-sermon and plaster starts landing in His hair.
Daylight. Then a hole. Then a mat on four ropes, coming down into the middle of the meeting.

And the Bible says, "When Jesus saw their faith…"
Their faith. Not the man on the mat's. Theirs.

Write these down:

Somebody's ceiling got ruined that day. Nobody's faith did.

A crowd at the door is not the end of the road. It's the reason God made stairs.

They brought him for his legs. Jesus started with his heart.

So today:
Scroll back to that name.
Pick your corner back up.
Pray like you're holding a rope.
And if you're the one on the mat right now — let somebody carry you.

Say it out loud:
"Lord, see our faith — and speak over the one I'm carrying: Son, thy sins be forgiven thee."

Go back up on the roof.
No ceiling is stronger than a friend who won't let go.

In Jesus' name.
