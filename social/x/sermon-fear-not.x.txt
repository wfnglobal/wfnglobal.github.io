POSTER CAPTION
There are 400 mentions of "fear" in the Bible, but only 62 times does heaven say "Fear not." So where did fear come from in the first place? Genesis 3:10 has the answer, and it'll change the way you read the whole Bible. 🧵👇

TWO-PARAGRAPH POST
Every time an angel shows up in Scripture, the first thing he says is "Fear not." To Mary, to the shepherds, and at the empty tomb, heaven opens with the same words. Why? Because fear wasn't part of God's original design. The first time anyone was afraid was in Genesis 3:10, right after a man heard God's voice and hid.

David didn't say "I don't feel afraid." He said "What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee." That's a decision, not a feeling. Read the full sermon, "Fear Not: Why Heaven Keeps Saying It," at wfnglobal.org/articles/fear-not.html
