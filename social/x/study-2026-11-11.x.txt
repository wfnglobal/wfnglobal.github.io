=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; post ~6–7 PM) ===
Lazarus, Come Forth

Jesus got word that His friend was dying.

He loved him. John says so — by name, three times, in one verse.

And He stayed exactly where He was. Two more days.

Not because He didn't care.

Because He wasn't racing to arrive before the funeral. He was aiming at something bigger than being on time.

By the time He reached Bethany, Lazarus had been in the grave four days.

Martha met Him on the road with grief dressed up as theology: "Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died."

Jesus didn't defend His timeline.

He said something stranger — a sentence that changes the tense of the whole conversation.
