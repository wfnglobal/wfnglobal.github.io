=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; post ~6–7 PM) ===
I Will Not Let Thee Go

The night before Jacob had to face Esau, he sent everything ahead of him.

His wives. His kids. His flocks. Every bribe he could scrape together.

The schemer who'd talked his way through his whole life had nothing left to negotiate with.

Then a Man grabs him in the dark, and they fight till dawn.

One touch, and Jacob's hip comes out of the socket. Just a touch. Which means the Man could've ended this anytime.

He says "let me go." Jacob says no.

Broken hip, exhausted, no honor at stake, full permission to quit — and he still won't let go.

"I will not let thee go, except thou bless me."

Here's what's wild: he never even got the Man's name.
