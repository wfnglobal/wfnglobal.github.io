=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; post ~6–7 PM) ===
Rhoda Left Him Standing at the Door

Herod put 16 soldiers and two chains on one fisherman.

Meanwhile the church was doing the only thing that actually mattered: praying for him without stopping.

The night before his execution, Peter was asleep between two guards. Not pacing. Asleep.

An angel hit him on the side to wake him up. The chains just fell off. No explanation given.

Peter walked right past the guards and out through a gate that opened by itself — and the whole time he thought he was dreaming.

Then he got to the prayer meeting that had been begging God for exactly this.

A girl named Rhoda heard his voice at the door, got so excited she forgot to actually open it, and ran to tell everyone.

Their response? "You're crazy." Then: "It's probably his ghost."
