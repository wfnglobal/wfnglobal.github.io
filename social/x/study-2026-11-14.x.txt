=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; post ~6–7 PM) ===
He Kept His Window Open

Daniel's rivals spent weeks digging through his whole career looking for one thing to use against him.

They found nothing. No error. No fault.

So they changed the crime: if his record was clean, they'd make his God illegal instead.

They got the king to sign a decree — thirty days, no prayers to anyone but him, penalty: lions.

Sealed under a law that even the king who signed it couldn't undo.

Daniel heard about it the same day it passed.

He went home anyway, to the room with the windows already open toward Jerusalem, and did exactly what he always did.

Three times a day. No hiding. No change in volume.
