=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; post ~6–7 PM) ===
Where Are Thine Accusers?

The religious crowd dragged a woman into the temple court, caught in the act, and set her in the middle for everyone to see.

They weren't actually after her.

She was bait. The real target was Jesus — "tempting him, that they might have to accuse him."

So He gives them the verdict format they asked for, with one condition attached: "He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her."

That's not Him softening the law. That's Him enforcing the part they conveniently left out.

Watch what happens next — the exits start with the oldest, not the youngest.

Apparently a longer career gives you a longer list of your own.
