=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; post ~6–7 PM) ===
The Woman Who Left Her Water Pot

She came to Jacob's well at noon.

Not morning. Not evening.

Noon — the hour with the fewest witnesses, because she'd built her whole schedule around not being seen.

Then a Jewish rabbi she'd never met asked her for a drink.

He broke two unwritten laws in one sentence, and He didn't lead with her file.

He led with a request she could actually say yes to.

She talked logistics. He talked thirst.

"Whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst."

Then He said five words that stopped the whole conversation cold:

"Go, call thy husband, and come hither."
