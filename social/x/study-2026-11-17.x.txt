=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; post ~6–7 PM) ===
They Thought She Was Drunk

Hannah went to the tabernacle that night in bitterness of soul, weeping, making a vow to God.

Her lips moved. No sound came out.

That's it. That's the whole prayer — just moving lips and a silence nobody could hear.

The priest Eli watched her from his seat by the door and reached the only conclusion his eyes could give him: she must be drunk.

"How long wilt thou be drunken? put away thy wine from thee."

A trained priest, standing feet from the realest thing happening in that building all week, completely misread it.

Hannah didn't argue theology. She just told him the truth in one sentence.

A thousand years later, a very different room would get accused of the exact same thing — at nine in the morning, for the exact opposite reason.
