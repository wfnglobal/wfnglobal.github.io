=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; post ~6–7 PM) ===
Make Me a Cake First

A widow was out gathering sticks for her last meal.

One handful of flour left. A little oil in a jar. After this, she and her son were going to die.

A prophet showed up out of nowhere and asked her for water.

Then bread.

She told him the truth — there wasn't enough to spare.

He said feed me anyway. First. Before you feed your own son.

Not after you're sure you'll be okay. First.

She went and did it with nothing left to lose.

What happened to that barrel next is the part most people skip straight past.
