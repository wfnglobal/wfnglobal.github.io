=== B) LONG POST (X Premium long-form; post ~6–7 PM) ===
Make Haste, and Come Down

He was rich. He was hated. And he was too short to see over a crowd.

So Zacchaeus did something no dignified rich man does.

He ran. He climbed a sycamore tree. Just for one glimpse of Jesus walking by.

Jesus stopped under that tree.

Looked up. And said his name.

Not "you up there." His actual name — in front of the whole crowd that despised him.

Then He said something that should've been impossible: "I must abide at thy house." Today.

The crowd started murmuring immediately…

=== D) STORY POST (X Premium long-form; standalone; post ~9–10 AM Toronto, ahead of the lunchtime poster) ===
Some of you have done really well for yourself.

Good job. Nice house. People need things from you now instead of the other way around.

And you are still, somehow, on the outside of everything that actually matters.

You scroll past other people's dinners, other people's laughter, other people's "we're-so-close" photos — and some quiet part of you climbs up into a tree just to watch from a safe distance.

You tell yourself: I don't really fit in that picture anyway.

That's a lie talking. Not your bank account. Not your face. Not your past. A lie.

I want to take you to Jericho.

There's a man there named Zacchaeus. Rich. Powerful. The most hated man in town, because his wealth came straight out of everyone else's pocket.

He hears Jesus is coming through, and he wants one thing: just to see Him. Not to talk to Him. Not to be known by Him. Just a look, from far away, the way everybody else got to see Him for free.

Problem is, he's short, and nobody in that crowd is making room for the man who's been quietly robbing them for years.

So he does something undignified. He runs ahead like a kid and climbs a tree.

And Jesus stops. Looks up. And says his name.

His actual name. In front of everyone who'd already decided exactly who he was.

"Zacchaeus — make haste, and come down. Today I'm coming to your house."

Here's what that tree is actually teaching you:

Watching from a distance was never the ceiling on your story — it was just where Jesus happened to find you.

You don't climb down to earn an invitation. You climb down because the invitation already had your name on it.

Jesus doesn't RSVP to your reputation. He invites Himself to the one house everybody said He shouldn't.

So get up.

Stop managing people's opinion of you from a safe, undignified distance.

Come down off whatever tree you climbed just to catch a glimpse of a life you've decided you don't deserve.

Say this out loud right now:

"Jesus, make haste and come to my house today — I'm done watching from a tree."

Climb down. He already knows your name, and He already decided you're worth the detour.

In Jesus' name.
